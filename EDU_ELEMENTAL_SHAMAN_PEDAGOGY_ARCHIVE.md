## 2. The "Ladder-Logic" Cognitive Architecture (1,000 Section-Finder Drills)
Traditional code exam prep fails experienced tradespeople because it forces rote memorization of isolated rules rather than training **rapid open-book navigation under timed exam conditions**.

Our **3-Rung Ladder-Logic Method** trains the student's brain to parse any exam sentence into three deterministic lookup steps:
- **🪜 Rung 1 (Macro Architecture — NEC Chapter 1 through 9)**:
  - *Chapters 1–4*: General Rules (Intro/Definitions/Installations ➔ Circuits/Loads/Grounding ➔ Wiring Methods/Conductors/Boxes ➔ General Equipment/Motors/Transformers).
  - *Chapters 5–7*: Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, and Special Conditions.
  - *Chapter 8*: Independent Communications Systems.
  - *Chapter 9*: Physical Tables (Conduit Fill, Conductor Properties).
- **🪜 Rung 2 (Physical Index Tab & Article Selection)**:
  - Maps the sentence's **Trigger Key Words** directly to one of the **36 Physical Index Tabs** on the student's tabbed code book.
- **🪜 Rung 3 (Exact Section & Table Pinpointing)**:
  - Locates the exact NEC Section (`e.g., 210.8(A), 240.4(B), 250.66, 310.16, 314.16(B), 430.52`) in under 15 seconds.
